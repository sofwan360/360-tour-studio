QUICK GUIDE

Build your first tour

  1. Import panoramas

    Add one 360° equirectangular JPG or PNG for each room.

  2. Connect your scenes

    Choose the + hotspot tool, then click inside the panorama. Select a destination scene in Properties.

  3. Add a floor plan

    Upload a plan and click to place the active scene marker.

  4. Test on a phone

    Open the published HTTPS link and tap the motion button. iPhone may ask for permission.

  5. Export

    Export a standalone HTML tour or a project backup JSON.

Panorama images should ideally have a 2:1 aspect ratio (for example, 6000 × 3000 px).
